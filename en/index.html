[F1] ABOUT THE GAME

A black screen, green monospaced text, box-drawing frames, a CRT case with rounded corners, phosphor glow and a rolling scan line. Under this shell is a full real-time-with-pause strategy game: you run a country day by day from 1 January 1962.

What awaits you

  • Two sides with different paths: the Ordensstaat Burgund absorbs the collapsing Reich, the Black League gathers Russia piece by piece.
  • A detailed map of Europe with the regions and borders of 1962: conquered lands pass to you region by region.
  • Dozens of events and superevents with ASCII animations and synthesised sound: sirens, marches, teletype, a mushroom cloud.
  • Army and diplomacy: conscription, exercises, factories, doctrines, talks, espionage, bribery, puppets.

And above all

  • A nuclear programme: uranium, facilities, leaks and accidents, the test, warheads, bombers and missiles.
  • Escalation: manoeuvres, border shootouts, false alarms, casus belli. The war moves along a front line and most often ends in a nuclear exchange.
  • Internal politics: factions, loyalty, purges, a «Night of the Long Knives».
  • Eight endings and an epilogue that retells your decisions.

[F2] STORY

Germany and Japan won the Second World War. For twenty years the Reich has ruled from the Atlantic to the Urals, but it is held together only by the name of one man. When he is gone, Europe will fall into the abyss, and someone has to reach the edge first.

  1. 1962The Reich looks invincible, but its economy is cracking, and the heirs are fighting around the ageing Führer: Bormann and the Party Chancellery, the reformer Speer with the industrialists, Reichsmarschall Göring with the army and the Luftwaffe, Heydrich and the SS.
  2. DEATH AT THE SUMMITAt 3:40 in the morning Adolf Hitler's heart stops. Berlin burns, the Reich breaks into shards, the Reichskommissariats are left without a master. The turmoil begins.
  3. WESTIn Brussels Reichsführer Heinrich Himmler has been waiting for this day. His Ordensstaat Burgund — an SS order-state and one vast concentration camp — sends its divisions into Germany, while the regular army marches into France.
  4. EASTIn Vologda, amid the ruins of Western Russia, the Black League has dug in — General Karbyshev's officers' clique, ruled by Supreme Commander Yazov. It will gather the atamanates, soviets, monarchies and enclaves by word or by fire.
  5. RACEBoth sides secretly build the bomb. Tension on the new border grows: manoeuvres, shootouts, false alarms. Neither Burgund nor the League accepts ultimatums.
  6. THE GREAT JUDGEMENTThe League believes Russia must come to the final war with the Germans united, indivisible and invincible. Who starts it first and what is left of Europe afterwards is up to you.

[F3] CHOOSE A SIDE

[1] ORDENSSTAAT BURGUND

CAPITAL: BRUSSELS

The SS Order-State of Reichsführer Heinrich Himmler: Belgium and north-eastern France.

The whole country is one vast concentration camp: factories and mines run on prisoner labour, an informer watches everyone, and the knights of the SS Order rule. When the Führer dies, Heydrich and the SS in Germany will wait for orders from Brussels.

STYLE OF PLAY: strong industry, few people. «Absorbing Germany» and «Conquest of France» open with the civil war in the Reich. The path ends with «The Fall of Berlin».

[2] BLACK LEAGUE

CAPITAL: VOLOGDA

An officers' clique founded by General Karbyshev and entrenched in Vologda amid the ruins of Western Russia. In practice it is ruled by Supreme Commander Dmitry Yazov.

The country has fallen apart into atamanates, soviets and enclaves. The League will gather them by word or by fire, and then lead them West. Its rivals are the Gorky Directorate, Rokossovsky in the Urals and Kirov's Tyumen Soviet.

STYLE OF PLAY: huge reserves, weak industry. «Unify Russia» is available almost at once. The path ends with «Banner over the Kremlin».
